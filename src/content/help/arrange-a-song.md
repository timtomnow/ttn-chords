Most songs repeat: verse, chorus, verse, chorus, bridge, chorus. Instead of typing
the chorus out four times, write it **once** as a part and then name it four times
in the **Arrangement**. Every occurrence shares the same chords and the same beat
timing, so tagging the chorus once times all four of them.

## Before you start

- Turn on [authoring (admin) mode](/help/turn-on-admin-mode).
- Add the parts you need first — the arrangement is built out of them.

## Steps

1. Open a song and tap **Edit**.
2. In the **Arrangement** card, tap **Build an arrangement**. It starts as your
   parts in their current order.
3. Tap **Add part** and choose a part to play it again.
4. Drag the chips to change the running order.
5. Tap a chip to set options for that one occurrence:
   - **Play** — repeat it back to back (once, 2×, 3×, …).
   - **Transpose** — take just this time through up or down, e.g. a last chorus a
     tone higher.
   - **Note for this time through** — a reminder like "quietly", shown only here.
   - **Remove** — drop this occurrence (the part itself stays).
6. Tap **Done**.

To go back to simply playing the parts in order, tap **Reset to parts order**.

## What you'll see

The song view, the reading views, the Highway, and printed reports all follow the
arrangement, numbering repeats as **Chorus 1**, **Chorus 2**, and so on. The parts
list below stays one entry per part — that's the part you edit.

Because occurrences share one part, editing its chords or beats changes every
place it's played. If you want one chorus to differ, make it a separate part.
