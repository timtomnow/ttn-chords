Most teaching material is regular: a chord every two beats, or one bar per line of
lyrics. **Space chords** fills in the whole song's timing from that pattern in one
tap, so the Highway view and play-along work without tagging anything by hand. Fix
only the spots that are genuinely irregular afterwards.

## Before you start

- Turn on [authoring (admin) mode](/help/turn-on-admin-mode).
- Set the song's **Time** and **Tempo (BPM)** first — spacing works in bars.

## Steps

1. Open a song and tap **Edit**.
2. In the **Beats** card, pick a **Spacing** style:
   - **Bar per line** — every line of lyrics starts on a downbeat. The best
     starting point for most songs.
   - **Straight through** — the same spacing from start to finish, ignoring line
     breaks.
   - **Follow the words** — chords land in proportion to where they sit in each
     line, so a chord over the last word arrives late in the bar.
3. Set **Beats per chord** (2 and 4 are the usual answers) and **Snap**.
4. Leave **Keep parts I've already timed** ticked so hand-tagged parts aren't
   flattened.
5. Tap **Space chords**. The line beside the button says how many chords in how
   many parts will change.

## What you'll see

Every chord in the affected parts gets an exact beat, and the song plays in the
**Highway** view straight away. To check and adjust, open **Tag beats** — its grid
also has a **Space** button, so you can space a part and then drag only the chords
that are off.

**Clear all beats** removes the timing again and leaves the chords and lyrics
alone. Parts written as a [chord grid](/help/chord-grid-parts) are timed by their
grid and are never respaced.
