Tagging beats records exactly when each chord lands, which is what the
[Highway view](/help/highway-view) needs to scroll in time. You tag by playing
along once, then fine-tune on a bar grid.

If the song is regular, [spacing the chords evenly](/help/space-chords-evenly) is
faster and often all you need — this guide is for songs that aren't.

## Before you start

- Turn on [authoring (admin) mode](/help/turn-on-admin-mode).
- If the song has more than one difficulty version, tag each version separately.

## Steps

1. Open a song and tap **Tag beats**.
2. Set the **Tempo**, then tap **Start**. A one-bar count-in plays.
3. On each beat a chord lands, tap the big button (or press **Space**, **B**, or
   **F**).
4. When you finish, fine-tune on the grid: drag a chord, **nudge** it
   earlier/later, adjust the snap (**1/4**, **1/8**, **1/16**), or insert and
   delete bars. You can also edit the words here — click a lyric to change or
   split it, or use **Lyric** to drop a word on its own beat. See
   [Edit chords and lyrics on the beat grid](/help/edit-on-the-grid).
5. Tap **Save** to store the timing.

## Reading the grid

- Bars wrap onto rows. Use **Bars per row** (2, 4, or 8) and the zoom buttons to
  fit a long song onto a small screen.
- Each row is labelled with the part that starts in it.
- Select a chord and its position shows in the toolbar as **bar _n_ beat _n_**.
- **Space** opens the even-spacing tool, so you can lay a part out in one go and
  then drag only the chords that are wrong.

Tagging works on each part once. A part the [arrangement](/help/arrange-a-song)
plays four times is tagged here once, and all four occurrences follow.

## What you'll see

Each chord gets an exact onset. The song will now play in the **Highway** view,
and you can re-open Tag beats any time to adjust.
