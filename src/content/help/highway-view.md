The Highway view lays the chords out left-to-right on a beat grid and scrolls
them past a fixed playhead in time with the metronome, so you can see what's
coming. The current beat is highlighted as a full-height column in your accent
colour, so it's easy to follow from across a room.

## Before you start

- The song needs beat timing. If it has none, the Highway shows a **Tag beats**
  prompt — see [Tag the beats of a song](/help/tag-beats).

## Steps

1. Open a song, tap **Perform**, and choose **Highway** from the view menu.
2. While stopped, scroll the song freely, or tap the ruler at the top to set the
   start point (a green marker).
3. Press the large **Play** button in the centre of the header to start. If a
   count-in is set, a count-in bar scrolls past first and the song follows
   without a break. The same button turns into **Pause**. Playback always
   begins from the start of the bar your start point is in, so the downbeat
   lands on beat 1.
4. Use the toolbar to:
   - **Jump to…** a part.
   - **Loop** the part at the start point.
   - Change the **Count-in** (Off, or 1–4 bars) and how many **Bars** are visible.
5. To hear the chords as well as the click, turn on **Play the chords** — see
   [Play the chords, not just the click](/help/chord-sounds).
6. To change the timing or words, tap **Edit** — see
   [Edit chords and lyrics on the beat grid](/help/edit-on-the-grid).

## What you'll see

The chords scroll so the current beat sits at the playhead. That beat is marked
by a full-height accent column running the whole depth of the view, and the
current bar's number is highlighted at the top. Chords and lyrics keep their own
plain background so they stay readable through the column. You can make the bar
numbers larger from the metronome settings — see
[Adjust the metronome while performing](/help/metronome-in-perform).

The count-in is a real bar on the road rather than a number over the top: it sits
immediately before your start point with its beats numbered, and it scrolls past
the playhead so the bar you're starting on is visibly coming at you. A badge in
the corner shows the count as well, for when the bars are zoomed narrow.

The **Play** button and the metronome button are separate: Play runs the song
(with its count-in), while the metronome button just clicks. Starting one stops
the other.
