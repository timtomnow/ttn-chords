A song holds its details (title, key, tempo, and so on) and a set of **parts** —
verses, choruses, intros — each with chords over lyrics. You can start blank or
paste existing ChordPro text.

## Before you start

- Turn on [authoring (admin) mode](/help/turn-on-admin-mode).
- Fill in the **Key** early if you can: it turns on one-tap chord suggestions.

## Steps

1. Open **Songs**.
2. To start blank, tap **New**. To bring in existing text, tap **Import**, paste
   ChordPro, and import it as a draft.
3. Fill in the details: **Title**, **Artist**, **Key**, **Capo**, **Tempo
   (BPM)**, **Time**, and **Tags**.
4. Type the lyrics of a part into the text box on the left.
5. Add the chords whichever way suits you:
   - Tap a chord in the row above the text box to drop it in at the cursor. The
     first chords are the ones already used in this song; after the divider are
     the chords that belong to the song's key.
   - Or tap a letter in the chart on the right to put a chord on that word. Tap
     a chord there to change or remove it, and drag one sideways to move it onto
     a different syllable.
   - Or type them by hand as `[G]Amazing [C]grace`.
6. Use **Add part** to add more, the kind menu to label them (Verse, Chorus, …),
   and the drag handle to reorder.

## What you'll see

The chart on the right always matches the text on the left — they are two views
of the same part, so edit in whichever one is quicker. Any symbol the app doesn't
recognise as a chord is flagged beneath the chart; that's fine for cues like
`N.C.`, and worth a second look otherwise.

Edits save automatically — a **Saved** indicator confirms. Tap **Done** to return
to the song, where you can perform it or work on its beats.

## Keyboard shortcuts

While the cursor is in a part's text box:

- **Alt + ↑ / ↓** — transpose the chord at the cursor up or down a semitone.
- **Alt + ← / →** — slide that chord one letter earlier or later.
- **Tab / Shift + Tab** — jump to the next or previous chord.
- **Ctrl + K** (**⌘ + K** on a Mac) — insert empty brackets ready to type into.
